🎲 TAMBOLA RULES & SETUP

1

📝 Keep Your Tickets Ready

Each player needs a Tambola ticket with 3 rows, 9 columns, and 15 numbers.

🛒 Buy Tickets on Flipkart
2

🎋 BAMBOO

First player to mark any 1 number on their ticket wins this round.

💰 Prize: Small prize/points
⏱️ Duration: ~5 seconds from first draw
3

🔲 CORNER

First player to mark all 4 corner numbers on their ticket wins.

📍 Corner Numbers: Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Left, Bottom-Right
💰 Prize: Medium prize
📊 Difficulty: ⭐⭐ (Medium)
4

✋ FIRST 5

First player to mark any 5 numbers anywhere on their ticket wins.

🎯 Pattern: Any 5 numbers (no specific order needed)
💰 Prize: Medium prize
📊 Difficulty: ⭐⭐ (Medium)
5

➡️ LINE / EARLY LINE

First player to mark all numbers in any 1 horizontal row on their ticket wins.

📐 Pattern: Complete any single row (left to right)
💰 Prize: Good prize
📊 Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐ (Hard)
💡 Tip: Early Line = First row completed (usually higher prize)
6

🏆 FULL HOUSE

First player to mark all 15 numbers on their ticket wins the JACKPOT!

🎯 Pattern: All 15 numbers across all 3 rows
💰 Prize: JACKPOT (Highest Prize!)
📊 Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Hard)
🎉 Celebration: Grand finale of the game
