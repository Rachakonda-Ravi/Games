📖 Rules

🎯 Objective
Reach square 100 before the other players.
🎲 Dice
Roll a standard six-sided die and move forward by the rolled number.
🪜 Ladders
Landing on the bottom of a ladder immediately moves your token to its top.
🐍 Snakes
Landing on a snake's head immediately sends your token down to its tail.
🏁 Winning
You must reach exactly 100. If your roll goes beyond 100, you stay where you are.
🧩 Random Board
Snakes and ladders are randomized for every game. No endpoint can be shared. Squares 1 and 100 are never endpoints.
